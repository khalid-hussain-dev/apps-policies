# Privacy Policy for Pro Document Scanner

Effective date: September 09, 2026

Developer: 13 Dimensions Studio  
Contact: 13dimensionsgraphics@gmail.com  
Website: https://13dimensions-studio.github.io/Official-Website/

This Privacy Policy explains how Pro Document Scanner ("App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the App.

## 1. Overview

Pro Document Scanner is an Android application designed for document scanning, PDF creation, merging, compression, OCR text extraction, and document organization. 

We prioritize your privacy: **all document scanning, image cropping, PDF processing, password encryption, and OCR text recognition are performed strictly on your local device.** Your documents, photos, and extracted text are stored in your device's local storage and are never uploaded, transmitted, or sold to our servers or any third-party servers.

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Account & Authentication Information
When you register or log into the App, we use **Google Firebase Authentication** to manage your account safely. The collected data includes:
- Email address
- User account ID (UID)
- Sign-in timestamp and verification status

This information is used strictly to identify your account, send verification emails, and manage your active subscription status.

### B. Subscription & Payment Information
Purchases and subscriptions (Monthly, Yearly, and Lifetime) are processed securely by **Google Play Billing**. 
- We **do not** collect or store any financial information (such as credit card numbers or billing addresses). All financial transactions are managed directly by Google Play.
- We store subscription verification metadata in Firebase solely to validate your active Pro status across devices.

### C. Advertising Data (Free Tier Only)
For users on the free tier, the App displays advertisements provided by **Google AdMob**. AdMob may collect and process:
- Device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID)
- Network state and approximate location (coarse level)
- Ad interaction and app performance telemetry

This data is processed in accordance with [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). **Pro users do not see advertisements and no ad tracking occurs on Pro accounts.**

### D. On-Device Machine Learning (OCR)
Our Optical Character Recognition (OCR) feature utilizes **Google ML Kit** to extract text from images. All image processing occurs locally on your hardware. No image contents or recognized text leave your device.

### E. App Permissions
To function properly, the App requests the following permissions:
- **Camera (`CAMERA`)**: Used exclusively to take pictures of physical documents for scanning inside the App.
- **Storage (`READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`, `WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`, `READ_MEDIA_IMAGES`, `MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`)**: Used to import photos/PDFs and save exported document files locally on your device.
- **Internet (`INTERNET`, `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`)**: Used for account authentication, subscription status validation, and serving advertisements to free users.

## 3. How We Use Your Information

We use the limited information collected to:
- Provide and maintain App functionality and user account access.
- Process local document operations on your device.
- Verify Pro subscription status and grant premium feature access.
- Deliver advertisements to non-Pro users via Google AdMob.
- Provide customer support and address inquiries.

## 4. Third-Party Services

The App relies on trusted third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:
- **Google Play Services & Billing**: [Google Play Terms](https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/)
- **Firebase Authentication**: [Google Firebase Privacy](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
- **Google AdMob**: [Google Advertising Policies](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads)

## 5. Data Retention & Account Deletion

- All created PDFs, scanned images, and text files remain stored on your device until you manually delete them or uninstall the App.
- Account information stored in Firebase is retained as long as your account is active. You may request account deletion at any time by contacting us at the email below.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Pro Document Scanner is safe for general audiences, but does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the date at the top of this page. Your continued use of the App after an updated Privacy Policy becomes effective means you accept the revised policy.
