# Privacy Policy for Tic-Tac-Toe

Effective date: March 29, 2026

Developer: 13 Dimensions Studio  
Contact: 13dimensionsgraphics@gmail.com  
Website: https://13dimensions-studio.github.io/Official-Website/

This Privacy Policy explains how **Tic-Tac-Toe** ("App") by **13 Dimensions Studio** ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the App.

## 1. Overview

Tic-Tac-Toe is a simple Android game. The game itself runs locally on your device. We do not require you to create an account or sign in to use the App.

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Information we do not directly collect

We do not directly collect or ask for:
- Your name
- Your phone number
- Your home address
- Your contacts
- Your photos or media files
- Your microphone recordings
- Your camera data
- Your precise GPS location

### B. Information processed locally on your device

To make the game work, the App processes gameplay-related information locally on your device during use, such as:
- Your game moves
- The current score
- The selected play mode (2 Players or Vs CPU)
- Music mute/unmute state during the session

In the current version of the App, this gameplay information is not sent to our own servers.

### C. Information collected by third-party advertising services

The App uses **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)** to show banner and interstitial advertisements. When ads are requested, loaded, or displayed, Google and its partners may collect or receive data from your device. This may include:
- Advertising identifiers and other device identifiers
- IP address
- Approximate location inferred from IP address or device signals
- App interactions and ad interactions
- Diagnostic, crash, and performance information
- Device, browser, network, and app version information

This third-party data processing is governed by Google's own privacy terms and policies.

### D. Information you provide if you contact us

If you contact us by email, we may receive:
- Your email address
- The contents of your message
- Any information you choose to include in that message

We use this information only to respond to your inquiry and provide support.

## 3. How We Use Information

We use information as follows:
- To operate the game on your device
- To display and measure ads through AdMob
- To maintain app functionality and stability
- To respond to support or business inquiries you send to us

## 4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share or allow processing of limited data in the following cases:
- With **Google AdMob** and related Google advertising services for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and ad personalization where applicable
- With your chosen email or browser app when you tap contact or website links
- When required by law, regulation, legal process, or enforceable governmental request

## 5. Permissions and Device Access

The App does not request dangerous runtime permissions such as access to your camera, microphone, contacts, or storage.

Because the App includes Google Mobile Ads, the final Android build includes network-related and advertising-related permissions required by that SDK, including internet access, network state access, and advertising ID-related permissions.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

We do not maintain user accounts for this App and we do not keep a personal user database for the App on our own servers.

If you contact us by email, we may retain that communication for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, follow up, keep business records, or comply with legal obligations.

Data processed by Google AdMob or other third-party services is retained according to those providers' own policies. You can manage certain advertising preferences through your Android device settings and your Google ad settings.

## 7. Security

Because the App does not operate its own user account system or backend database, we do not store personal account records for App users on our own servers.

We rely on Android platform protections and the security practices of our service providers, such as Google, for network and advertising-related processing. No method of electronic transmission or storage is guaranteed to be 100% secure.

## 8. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through the App.

However, third-party advertising providers used in the App may process device-related data as described above. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that personal information has been provided to us directly, please contact us.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will post the updated version and revise the effective date at the top of this document.
