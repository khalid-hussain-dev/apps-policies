# Privacy Policy for Slither Saga

Effective date: March 29, 2026

Developer: 13 Dimensions Studio  
Contact: 13dimensionsgraphics@gmail.com  
Website: https://13dimensions-studio.github.io/Official-Website/

This Privacy Policy explains how 13 Dimensions Studio ("we," "our," or "us") handles information in connection with **Slither Saga** (the "App"). By using the App, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

## 1. Summary

Slither Saga is a mobile game. The current release does not require you to create an account or submit personal information to play. The App stores limited gameplay data on your device, such as your high score, and uses Google AdMob to display ads. Google AdMob may automatically collect certain device and usage data to serve ads, measure performance, and help prevent fraud.

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Information stored locally on your device

- Your high score is stored locally on your device so it can be shown again the next time you play.
- This local game data may also be included in Android backup and restore features if backup is enabled on your device/account.

### B. Information collected automatically by third-party advertising services

The App uses Google AdMob to show banner and interstitial ads. Depending on your device, region, and Google ad settings, Google and its partners may automatically collect and process information such as:

- IP address
- Advertising ID and other device or account identifiers
- App launch and ad interaction data, such as taps and views
- Diagnostic and performance information
- General location inferred from IP address

### C. Information you choose to provide

If you contact us by email, we may receive the information you include in your message, such as your email address, name, and the contents of your support request.

## 3. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect Directly

The App does not require account registration and does not request direct access to your contacts, camera, microphone, photos, or precise location in the current release.

## 4. How We Use Information

We use information handled in connection with the App to:

- Provide gameplay features, including saving and showing your high score
- Display ads and support monetization of the App
- Measure ad performance and app stability
- Detect, prevent, or respond to fraud, abuse, or technical issues
- Respond to support requests or feedback you send to us

## 5. Sharing of Information

We may share or allow sharing of information in the following circumstances:

- With Google AdMob and related Google services to serve ads, measure performance, and support fraud prevention
- With service providers or infrastructure providers who help us operate, maintain, or secure the App or our communications
- When required by law, legal process, or to protect rights, safety, and the integrity of the App
- In connection with a business transfer such as a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets

## 6. Third-Party Services

The App uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob). Google may collect, use, and share data under its own policies. You can review Google's policies here:

- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

- Local gameplay data such as your high score remains on your device until you clear the App's data, uninstall the App, or restore or replace your device.
- If Android backup is enabled, backed up app data may remain in your Google backup history according to your device and Google account settings.
- Support emails or related correspondence may be kept for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, resolve issues, maintain records, or meet legal obligations.
- Data handled by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's own policies and retention practices.

## 8. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information associated with the App. Local gameplay data is stored in app storage on your device. According to Google, data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## 9. Your Choices

- You can stop all collection by uninstalling the App.
- You can remove local gameplay data by clearing the App's storage through your Android device settings.
- You may be able to reset or delete your advertising ID and manage ad personalization through your Android or Google settings.
- You can choose whether to contact us by email and what information to include.

## 10. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information through a support email or other direct contact, please contact us and we will review the request and take appropriate action.
