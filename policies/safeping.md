# Privacy Policy for SafePing

Effective date: March 27, 2026

Developer: 13 Dimensions Studio  
Contact: 13dimensionsgraphics@gmail.com  
Website: https://13dimensions-studio.github.io/Official-Website/

This Privacy Policy applies to the current free version of SafePing for Android. It explains how SafePing accesses, stores, uses, and shares data when you use the app. SafePing does not require you to create an account. If future updates, including Pro features, materially change how data is handled, this Privacy Policy will be updated.

## 1. Overview

- **App Name**: SafePing
- **Developer**: 13 Dimensions Studio
- **Contact Email**: 13dimensionsgraphics@gmail.com
- **Website**: https://13dimensions-studio.github.io/Official-Website/

## 2. Information SafePing Accesses and Uses

SafePing may access or use the following information:
- Contact names and phone numbers that you manually enter into the app
- Message template titles and message contents that you manually create in the app
- The contact and template selections you mark as currently selected
- Basic network status information to determine whether to use online mode or offline SMS mode
- A limited check of whether WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business is installed on your device so the app can open a chat when you choose online mode
- Advertising-related technical data collected by Google Mobile Ads SDK, as described below

SafePing does not currently request access to your device contact list, call log, camera, microphone, or location.

## 3. How SafePing Uses This Information

SafePing uses information:
- To save your contacts and message templates inside the app
- To let you choose regular and emergency contacts and templates
- To send alerts that you start yourself, either through WhatsApp or SMS
- To display ads and support the free version of the app
- To detect whether internet connectivity is available
- To let you contact the developer by email or visit the developer website if you tap those links

## 4. Where Your Data Is Stored

The contacts, phone numbers, template titles, template messages, and selection state you enter are stored locally in SafePing's on-device SQLite database.

SafePing does not currently use its own backend account system or cloud database for its core alert, contact, and template features.

Depending on your device settings, Android may back up app data for restore or device transfer purposes.

## 5. When Data Is Shared

SafePing shares or transmits data only in the following situations:
- **SMS mode**: If you choose offline SMS mode, the selected phone number or phone numbers and message content are sent through your mobile carrier or SMS provider.
- **WhatsApp mode**: If you choose online mode, SafePing opens WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business with the selected phone number and message content using a chat link. Data handled there is subject to WhatsApp's terms and privacy practices.
- **Ads**: SafePing uses Google Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). According to Google's official developer documentation, the SDK automatically collects and shares data such as IP address, user product interactions, diagnostic information, and device and account identifiers, including Android advertising ID and app set ID, for advertising, analytics, and fraud prevention.
- **Connectivity check**: SafePing may make a lightweight request to a Google connectivity endpoint to determine whether internet access is available.
- **Email or browser actions**: If you tap email or website links, the relevant email app or browser app will handle that interaction under its own privacy practices.

We do not sell your personal data.

## 6. Ads and Third-Party Services

SafePing uses Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK to show banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads in the free version of the app.

Third-party privacy resources:
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
- [Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosure](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/privacy/play-data-disclosure)
- [WhatsApp Privacy Policy](https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/privacy-policy)

We do not control the privacy practices of third-party services after you leave SafePing or interact with those services directly.

## 7. Permissions

SafePing currently uses these Android permissions:
- `INTERNET` and `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` to load ads, open web-based features, and check internet connectivity
- `SEND_SMS` to send SMS alerts only when you use that feature

The app also checks whether WhatsApp or WhatsApp Business is installed so it can open the online messaging flow when you choose that feature.

## 8. Data Retention and Deletion

Data you enter into SafePing remains on your device until it is removed.

The current free version does not provide a dedicated in-app account or account deletion flow because SafePing does not require an account.

You can remove locally stored SafePing data by:
- Clearing the app's storage in Android system settings
- Uninstalling the app

If your device backup settings are enabled, backup copies may remain available until removed or overwritten under your Google or Android backup settings.

If you email the developer, your email address and any information you include in that message may be retained as needed to respond to you and maintain normal support records.

## 9. Security

- SafePing stores its core user-entered data locally on the device
- SafePing sends messages only when you trigger them through the app
- Google states that data collected by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit using TLS
- Standard SMS messages are not end-to-end encrypted

No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we aim to limit data handling to what is needed for the app's functionality.

## 10. Children's Privacy

SafePing is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through SafePing, please contact us and we will review the request.
