# Privacy Policy for Ledgerly

Effective date: September 14, 2026

Developer: 13 Dimensions Studio  
Contact: 13dimensionsgraphics@gmail.com  
Website: https://13dstudio.vercel.app/

This Privacy Policy explains how Ledgerly ("App", "we", "us", or "our") by 13 Dimensions Studio handles information when you use the App.

## 1. Overview

Ledgerly is an offline personal expense-tracking application for Android. The App runs locally on your device and does not require account creation, login, or internet access to track your expenses.

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Information stored locally on your device
Ledgerly allows you to enter and store:
- Expense titles, amounts, categories, dates, and optional notes.
- An optional profile name.
- Your application preferences, such as dark mode toggle, monthly budget, and daily reminder settings.

This information is stored locally on your device in Ledgerly's private database and shared preferences.

### B. Information we do not collect
In the current version, Ledgerly does **not**:
- Send expense records or profile information to any remote server.
- Use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
- Collect contacts, precise location, microphone recordings, camera feeds, or device identifiers for tracking.
- Require an active internet connection.

## 3. Notifications and Reminders

If you enable the Daily Log Reminder feature, Ledgerly schedules a local notification reminder directly on your device. Android notification permission is requested when required by your operating system. Reminder and budget alert notifications are generated locally by the App. You can disable reminders at any time in Settings or through Android system notification controls.

## 4. CSV Export and Sharing

Ledgerly creates a CSV file export only when you explicitly select the export option. Android then opens the system share sheet. If you share or transfer the exported file to another application, service, or individual, that recipient's privacy policy and practices will apply. Ledgerly does not automatically upload or transmit your CSV exports.

## 5. Backups and Device Transfers

Ledgerly excludes its local database and application preferences from automatic OS cloud backups and device-transfer backups to ensure your personal financial records remain under your direct control. Use the manual CSV export feature if you wish to transfer records to another device.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Your expense records remain stored on your device until you manually delete them inside Ledgerly, clear the App's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the App. Individual expense items can be deleted directly within the App. Exported CSV files remain wherever you choose to save or share them and must be deleted separately.

## 7. Third-Party Services and Links

The App's Contact Us section may provide links to the developer's website, email client, and Google Play developer page. Opening these links transfers you to third-party applications or services operating under their own privacy policies.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Ledgerly is a general-audience expense tracking tool and does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in App functionality or legal requirements. When updated, the effective date at the top of this policy will be revised accordingly.
