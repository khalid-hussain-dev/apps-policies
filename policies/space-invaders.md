# Privacy Policy for Space Invaders

Effective date: March 29, 2026

Developer: 13 Dimensions Studio  
Contact: 13dimensionsgraphics@gmail.com  
Website: https://13dimensions-studio.github.io/Official-Website/

This Privacy Policy explains how Space Invaders ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use the Android game **Space Invaders**.

This policy reflects the App as it exists today. If a future update adds features such as cosmetics, accounts, in-app purchases, cloud saving, or other online systems, this policy may be updated before those features are released.

## 1. Overview

Space Invaders is a classic retro arcade game for Android. High score and gameplay state stay on your device, with third-party Google AdMob serving advertisements.

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Information stored locally on your device
The App currently stores limited gameplay data locally on your device, including:
- Your high score

This data is used only to save your progress between play sessions.

### B. Information collected automatically by third-party advertising services
The App uses **Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK** to display banner ads and interstitial ads. As a result, Google and its partners may automatically collect certain information, which can include:
- IP address
- Advertising ID or other device/account identifiers
- App set ID
- Basic app interaction data, such as app launches, taps, and ad interactions
- Diagnostic and performance information
- Approximate location derived from IP address

This information may be collected for purposes such as advertising, ad measurement, analytics, fraud prevention, and service improvement.

### C. Information you choose to provide
If you contact us by email, we may receive the information you include in your message, such as:
- Your email address
- Your name, if you include it
- Any other information you choose to share

## 3. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect Directly

The App does not currently require account creation and does not directly ask you to provide:
- Your full name
- Your phone number
- Your precise location
- Your contacts
- Your photos or media files
- Microphone or camera data
- Payment card information

The App also does not currently include in-app purchases or user account registration.

## 4. How We Use Information

We use information to:
- Run the game and save your high score locally
- Show ads in the App
- Maintain, improve, and troubleshoot the App
- Respond to support messages or questions you send us

## 5. Permissions Used by the App

The current Android build uses network-related permissions and advertising-related permissions. These are used to:
- Load and display ads
- Check network availability for ad services
- Support advertising and attribution features provided by the Google Mobile Ads SDK
- Open our website or your email app when you choose those options

The App does **not** request sensitive runtime permissions such as camera, microphone, contacts, or precise location access.

## 6. Local Storage and Android Backup

The App stores your high score in private app storage on your device.

Because Android backup is enabled in the current build, some locally stored app data may also be included in Android backup or device-to-device restore features, depending on your device, Android version, and backup settings.

You can remove local game data by:
- Using the in-game reset option for progress/high score
- Clearing the App's storage in Android settings
- Uninstalling the App

## 7. Third-Party Services

The App currently uses the following third-party service:
- **Google AdMob / Google Mobile Ads SDK** for advertising

Google may process data according to its own privacy terms and policies. You can review Google's Privacy Policy here:
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

Please note that third-party services operate under their own privacy policies, and we do not control their independent data handling practices.

## 8. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

However, data may be shared with third-party service providers when necessary to operate the App, especially advertising services such as Google AdMob. This may include device identifiers, IP address, app interaction data, and diagnostic information processed through the ad SDK.

## 9. Data Retention

We retain data as follows:
- Local high score data remains on your device until you reset it, clear app storage, or uninstall the App
- Emails or support messages may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, keep records, or resolve issues
- Data collected by third-party ad providers is retained according to their own policies

## 10. Your Choices

You can:
- Stop using the App at any time
- Reset local game progress inside the App
- Clear app storage or uninstall the App to remove locally stored data
- Manage ad personalization, advertising ID settings, or related privacy controls through your Android and Google account settings, where available
- Manage your device backup settings through Android settings

## 11. Children's Privacy

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through account registration or similar features because the App does not currently provide those features.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information to us by email or another direct contact method, please contact us at [13dimensionsgraphics@gmail.com](mailto:13dimensionsgraphics@gmail.com), and we will review the request.
