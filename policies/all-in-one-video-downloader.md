# Privacy Policy for All-In-One Video Downloader

Effective date: March 28, 2026

Developer: 13 Dimensions Studio  
Contact: 13dimensionsgraphics@gmail.com  
Website: https://13dimensions-studio.github.io/Official-Website/

This Privacy Policy explains how All-In-One Video Downloader ("the App", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use the current free version of All-In-One Video Downloader.

This version of the App lets users analyze public links from supported platforms, view available download options, save media to their device, optionally create an account, and view local download history. If we add Pro features, subscriptions, or payment processing in a future release, we will update this Privacy Policy before those features go live.

## 2. Information We Collect

We collect different types of information depending on how you use the App.

### A. Information you provide directly

If you create an account, you may provide:
- Your name
- Your email address
- Your password

Authentication is handled through Firebase Authentication. We do not store your password in plain text inside the App.

If you contact us by email, we will receive any information you include in your message.

### B. Account and cloud profile information

If you sign in, the App may store or sync the following account-related information using Firebase services:
- Your email address
- Your display name
- Your Firebase user ID
- Account creation timestamp
- Account status fields used to support features such as entitlement or Pro access flags

At the time of this policy, the App syncs a basic user profile and stores an `isPro` status flag for signed-in users.

### C. Download and history information stored on your device

The App stores download history locally on your device. This may include:
- Platform name
- Video or post title
- Selected format label
- Saved file name
- Source URL
- Download timestamp
- Whether the history belongs to a guest session or signed-in account on that device

This history is currently stored locally in the App's local database on your device. It is not uploaded to our own download servers because we do not operate a separate download-history backend for this feature.

### D. Files and documents you choose to use

If you use the App's import/export features or download media, the App may process:
- Media files saved to your device's Downloads folder
- CSV backup files that you choose to import
- PDF history exports that the App creates at your request

History exports can include your account label, download titles, file names, source links, formats, and timestamps.

### E. Link analysis and third-party content requests

When you paste a supported link into the App, the App sends requests from your device to the relevant third-party platform or content host, such as YouTube, Facebook, Instagram, or their related content delivery networks, in order to:
- Read publicly available metadata
- Retrieve available media formats
- Start the download you requested

We do not run a separate media-processing server for this workflow in the current free version. These requests are made from your device to those third-party services.

### F. Advertising and device-related information

The App uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads.

According to Google's Mobile Ads SDK documentation, the SDK may automatically collect and share data such as:
- IP address
- Device and account identifiers, including advertising-related identifiers
- User interactions with ads and the app
- Diagnostic and performance information

This data may be used by Google and its partners for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and ad delivery purposes under their own policies.

### G. System backups

Depending on your Android device settings, some App data stored on your device may be included in Android system backup or device transfer features controlled by the operating system or your Google account.
