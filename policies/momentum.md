# Privacy Policy for Momentum

Effective date: September 14, 2026

Developer: 13 Dimensions Studio  
Contact: 13dimensionsgraphics@gmail.com  
Website: https://13dstudio.vercel.app/

This Privacy Policy explains how Momentum ("App", "we", "us", or "our") by 13 Dimensions Studio handles information when you use the App.

## 1. Overview

Momentum is an offline task-management application for Android. We do not operate user accounts and do not collect, sell, or share personal information through the App.

## 2. Information We Collect

### A. Information stored locally on your device
Momentum stores the following information locally on your device:
- Tasks that you create, including title, due date, reminder time, category, priority, completion status, and creation order.
- Your selected theme preference (light mode or dark mode).

This information is used strictly to provide task management, search, sorting, theme, and reminder functionality. The App does not send this data to remote servers.

### B. Information we do not collect
Momentum does **not** use accounts, advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, tracking technologies, or remote cloud databases. The App does not request access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos, or device files.

## 3. Notifications and Reminders

If you create a task reminder, Momentum schedules a local notification on your device. On Android 13 and later, the App requests the `POST_NOTIFICATIONS` runtime permission before displaying notification alerts. Notification permission is optional; denying permission prevents reminder notifications from appearing but does not affect other App features.

## 4. Backups and Device Transfers

Android may back up or transfer Momentum's local app data according to your operating system and Google account backup settings. These backups are managed by Android and your device settings. If you do not wish for app data to be backed up, you can adjust your Android device backup controls.

## 5. Third-Party Links and External Services

The App provides optional links to our developer website, email contact address, Google Play developer page, and public Privacy Policy directory. Tapping these links opens external applications or web browsers governed by their own privacy terms.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete individual tasks directly within Momentum at any time. Uninstalling the App removes its local storage from your device, subject to Android OS backup settings.

## 7. Security

Momentum retains all task data locally on your device. While we take standard precautions, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Momentum is designed to function without collecting personal identifiable information and is not directed specifically to children under the age of 13.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the App's data practices change or for legal/regulatory updates. Revised policies will include an updated effective date.
